Return:
- post_summary: one or two sentences on what the author is actually saying.
- detected_language: en, hi, hinglish, or other. Hinglish means Hindi written in Roman script, usually mixed with English.
- detected_tone: a few words, for example "excited, technical", "frustrated", "teaching".
- author_intent: what the author wants: teach, share a win, ask for help, start a debate, promote, entertain, or similar.
- topics: 1 to 5 short topic labels.
- technical_concepts: specific tools, techniques, or concepts mentioned. Empty list if none.
- invites_discussion: true if the item asks for or naturally opens a conversation.
- scores, each a number from 0.0 to 1.0:
  - topic: fit with Curiox topics (software development, AI/automation, fintech/trading tech, startups, tech creators). Off-topic is 0.
  - audience: how likely the readers are developers, builders, traders, founders, or tech creators.
  - conversation: how much room there is for a reply that adds something real.
  - quality: substance and care in the item itself. Low-effort, recycled, or clickbait content scores low.
- reject_reason: exactly one of
  none, giveaway, engagement_bait (e.g. "comment YES", "tag 3 friends", "drop a 🔥"), irrelevant_promo,
  sensitive (politics, religion, tragedy, health crises, personal hardship, specific stock or trade tips to individuals, or anything where a brand reply would be unwelcome),
  no_value (nothing meaningful could be added), spam.
  Use none when the item is fine.
- relevance_reason: one sentence explaining the scores to the human reviewer.

Be strict. Most items should not get a brand comment.
